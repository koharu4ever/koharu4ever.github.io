# Authorship badge frame

`handmade-frame.svg` is a local derivative assembled from two CC0 references:

- Kenney, **Medals 1.1**: <https://kenney.nl/assets/medals>
- Amousey, **Blue profile frame transparent.svg**: <https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Blue_profile_frame_transparent.svg>

Both sources are released under the Creative Commons CC0 1.0 Universal Public
Domain Dedication. The local derivative enlarges the portrait opening, shortens
and recolors the medal ribbons, combines the circular frame geometry, and adds
site-specific highlights and an empty label plate.

The portrait displayed inside the frame is supplied separately by the site
owner and is not covered by the frame sources' CC0 dedication.
